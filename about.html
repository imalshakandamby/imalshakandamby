About Me

Designing with intent,
not just intuition.

I'm Imalsha – a Product Designer based in the Netherlands who believes the best design is the kind people never have to think about.

For the past four years I've worked end to end across SaaS, B2B, D2C, AI-powered platforms, e-Commerce, web and mobile from the first user interview through to post-handoff design review. I've owned products solo and collaborated within larger teams, always with the same goal: close the gap between what a product promises and what a person actually experiences. I close that gap with research, clear information architecture, and design systems that survive contact with real engineering teams.

Where I've Worked

Experience that shipped

Four years across product design and frontend-adjacent engineering - building for real users, real deadlines and real teams.

Serving Australian & Global Clients

  • Promoted based on KPI performance for consistent ownership and delivery across a project-based environment
  • Shipped 28+ projects across 15+ domains simultaneously without losing speed or quality - including complex consumer apps and internal operational tools.
  • Built a token-driven design system of 48 components from scratch, adopted across 4 products, cutting handoff time by 40%
  • Owned the full design lifecycle end-to-end - from discovery and research through to post-handoff design review - across web, mobile, and AI-powered platforms
  • Collaborated with Product Managers, Developers, Test Engineers, Stakeholders, and Marketing teams to deliver UI/UX, branding assets, campaign materials, and social media content.

Serving Indian & Global Clients

  • Promoted within 3 months for switching between unrelated client domains quickly - learning each business context, adapting design decisions, and maintaining quality without losing speed.
  • Designed and shipped Shopify product recommendation quizzes across 5+ e-commerce domains - sitting directly in the purchase funnel, where better UX translates into measurable gains in discovery, personalization, and conversion.
  • Bridged design and development by building in Figma and implementing in HTML, CSS, and Shopify Liquid - fewer lost-in-translation moments, faster iterations, and a final product that actually matched the design intent.
  • Collaborated with Developers, Test Engineers, Project Managers, and Clients across multiple Shopify stores to ensure seamless implementation, design consistency, and high-quality delivery throughout.

Internship during University

  • Built responsive interfaces using React, Bootstrap, and Ant Design during a 6-month front-end internship.
  • Gained hands-on understanding of how design decisions behave in real code - spacing, component states, responsive breakpoints, and rendering constraints.
  • That early technical grounding has made every developer conversation since faster, more precise, and less likely to end in "that’s not possible."
  • Applied UI/UX principles directly in front-end implementation, not just in design files.
  • Developed early fluency across both design and engineering - understanding how the two disciplines speak differently about the same problem.
  • The result: cleaner handoffs, shorter feedback loops, and a significantly smaller gap between what gets designed and what actually gets shipped.

Core Skills

  • Sole Designer Ownership

    I've taken products from zero to shipped entirely alone - discovery, research, design system, UI, developer handoff and post-handoff review - without a safety net.

  • Systems Thinking at Scale

    I design components, not screens - building token-driven systems that give engineering teams a foundation they can extend without breaking consistency.

  • Research-to-Pixel Ownership

    I run my own discovery, synthesize findings, and translate them into interfaces - without needing a researcher to hand me insights before I can start.

  • AI-Native Product Design

    I design the interfaces where AI does the work - conversational flows, agentic interfaces, and trust-building interaction patterns across 10+ AI-powered platforms.

  • Cross-Domain Adaptability

    I've shipped across 18+ domains without a learning curve that costs the team - healthcare one sprint, fintech the next, same delivery standard throughout.

  • Design-Engineering Bridge

    I write HTML and CSS, speak developer fluently, and stay involved post-handoff - which means what gets designed and what gets shipped are usually the same thing.

  • UX Writing Embedded in Design

    The words inside a product are a design decision - I write conversational AI copy, onboarding flows, microcopy, and error states as part of the design process - not after it.
